Meet Kaiplan

Full description

Organize your routine simply, practically, and efficiently with Kaiplan.

Kaiplan brings calendars, appointments, tasks, and lists together in one place, helping you plan your day, track your goals, and stay organized.

View your schedule by day, week, or month, create events, organize tasks, track overdue activities, and clearly see what really matters. Customize your experience with themes, colors, and categories to fit the way you organize.

Tasks can be arranged in custom lists, with or without a set time, so you can distinguish scheduled appointments from activities you want to complete during the day. Track progress by marking tasks as complete whenever needed.

Kaiplan was built with privacy and performance in mind. Your main planning data remains on your device, providing fast access and greater control. You can also create and restore backups whenever you choose.

The app is continuously evolving, and new features will be added over future releases. Your suggestions, ideas, and bug reports help make Kaiplan increasingly complete and useful.

Plan better. Organize clearly. Make the most of every moment with Kaiplan.
