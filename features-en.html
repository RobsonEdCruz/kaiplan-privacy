Everything in one place

Main features

Tools to organize appointments and tasks while keeping things light.

Smart home

  • Today's appointments
  • Today's and overdue tasks
  • Upcoming days and recent completions

Calendar

  • Day and three-day views
  • Workdays and full week
  • Monthly calendar

Tasks and subtasks

  • Organization by lists
  • Complete and reopen items
  • Subtasks for each activity

Customization

  • Ready-made themes
  • Categories and tags
  • Color palette for cards

Reminders

  • Local notifications per item
  • Configurable daily summary
  • Times between 5 a.m. and 8 a.m.

Local backup

  • JSON export
  • Sharing through your device
  • Restore under your control

Google Calendar

  • Free connection to your Google Account
  • Select the calendars you want to follow
  • Periodic automatic synchronization
  • Google events in read-only mode

Planned for Premium

Future features

Automatic cloud backup, advanced recurrence, additional calendars, attachments, Google Calendar event editing, and advanced customization are planned and are not part of the currently available version.
